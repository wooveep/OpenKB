# Deprecate published knowledge before permanent deletion

The normal removal action for a published Knowledge Page is Knowledge
Deprecation: the page leaves default retrieval but retains its stable identity,
content, links, and revision history for explanation and OKF export. A separate
confirmed permanent-delete action remains available for a local user who
intentionally wants the page and its revision history removed; deleted review
candidates continue to follow their existing immediate-discard semantics.
