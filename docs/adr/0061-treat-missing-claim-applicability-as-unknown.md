# Treat missing claim applicability as unknown

OpenKB derives product version, platform, deployment scenario, and time applicability only from explicit evidence or reliable document metadata. A missing dimension is stored as Unspecified Applicability rather than treated as universal or inherited from recency and neighboring text. Non-conflicting unspecified guidance may remain visibly unqualified, but incompatibility with an explicitly scoped claim enters review. This avoids invented precision and unsafe generalization at the cost of retaining visible uncertainty.
