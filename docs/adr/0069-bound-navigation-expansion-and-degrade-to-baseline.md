# Bound navigation expansion and degrade to baseline

OpenKB performs Knowledge Navigation as deterministic orchestration inside `DesktopRetrievalService.retrieve`, reusing the optional Retrieval Plan, Catalog, FTS, knowledge-source resolution, and PageTree instead of adding a tool-using Wiki Agent or another model role. After query-scoped routing, one answer may read at most four combined Document Summaries or knowledge pages, two Source Read Windows, one resolvable Knowledge Link hop, and one Answer Coverage Gate-authorized PageTree supplement. A Source Read Window contains the locator block and adjacent structural context, initially capped at 3,000 characters; only sources of at most 6,000 normalized characters may be read in full, and selected EvidenceRefs rather than duplicate window text enter the answer prompt.

The answer Prompt Contract enforces a model-capacity-aware Grounding Budget, preserves its 2,048-token output reserve, bounds Conversation Context, and applies its 70% document-input share. Source Evidence receives at least 75% of that document budget and Knowledge Guidance at most 25%; an unknown model capacity uses a conservative code-owned fallback. If navigation, optional model work, budgeting, or expansion fails, retrieval returns the verified baseline Evidence Pack with an explicit degradation instead of blocking the answer, retrying without limit, or entering an open-ended loop. This makes the new path improve quality opportunistically while preserving predictable cost, latency, and availability.
