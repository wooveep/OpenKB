# Render kind-specific readable knowledge pages

Generated and user-owned knowledge follow a kind-specific content contract instead of a universal prose template or raw claim list. Entity, Concept, and Procedure pages expose structures appropriate to their meaning, omit empty sections, combine equivalent claims with all supporting sources, integrate complementary claims into coherent prose, and label applicability differences explicitly. Every factual paragraph or list item retains a compact claim-level source marker that opens its document, section, page or coordinate, and excerpt, while repeated sources are deduplicated in a page summary. This trades a more constrained synthesis and presentation contract for pages that remain readable without sacrificing evidence inspection.
