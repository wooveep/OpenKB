# Use one queryable subject or goal per knowledge identity

OpenKB admits a knowledge candidate only when it is independently queryable, reusable, and supported by a definition, operational goal, or substantive evidence-backed claim. One reusable Concept or Entity subject, or one user-completable Procedure goal, receives one corpus identity; source headings and subordinate details become sections or claims rather than pages. Canonical titles prefer official evidence-backed names and retain only supported or deterministic aliases, while automatic semantic identity matching requires the same kind, at least two independent non-model signals, a confirming structured model result, and no contrary evidence. This rejects noisy literals and source-shaped fragmentation at the cost of sending uncertain matches to review.
