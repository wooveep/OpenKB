# Separate OKF compatibility from publication eligibility

OpenKB validates projected bundles in two layers: a permissive OKF
Compatibility Lint follows the format's minimal conformance and preserves
unknown fields and broken links, while an independent Publication Gate
requires resolvable EvidenceRefs in Available Knowledge, an eligible Knowledge
Lifecycle, and a generation-consistent snapshot before content can contribute
to answers. This keeps exported knowledge interoperable without weakening
OpenKB's evidence and availability guarantees or misrepresenting local policy
as a universal OKF requirement.
