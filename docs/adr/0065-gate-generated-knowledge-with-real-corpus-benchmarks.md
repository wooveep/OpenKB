# Gate generated knowledge with real-corpus benchmarks

A candidate Generated Knowledge Generation must pass a fixed real-corpus evaluation before activation: every published factual unit has valid Evidence; labeled noise leakage is at most 2%; duplicate identity rate is at most 5%; at least 85% of eligible multi-document topics use evidence from two or more documents; benchmark Procedures cover at least 85% of their evidence-supported key stages; the dual-node hyperconverged deployment question passes completeness, correctness, and citation checks; and existing retrieval replays and automated tests do not regress. Knowledge Navigation acceptance additionally requires source-bound claims, operation and Grounding Budget compliance, exact baseline fallback behavior, and no ephemeral query-time knowledge pages. Portable Wiki fixtures must validate every route, link, alias, resource checksum, and snapshot identity before publication. These measures favor directly observed knowledge and answer quality over matching the original project's page counts or prose length.
